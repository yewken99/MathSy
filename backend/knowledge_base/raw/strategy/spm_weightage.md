## Concept: Revision Priority Based on Combined Analysis
The combined analysis of SPM $2021$ to SPM $2024$ suggests that students should balance revision between high-frequency chapters and personal weak topics.

**Key Rules / Methods:**
- First priority should be given to chapters that are both high-ranked and repeated in Kertas $2$.
- Second priority should be given to chapters that appeared in SPM $2024$ but had lower average ranking in earlier years.
- Third priority should be given to lower secondary foundation topics because they may appear inside Form $4$ and Form $5$ questions.
- Students should not revise based only on frequency because SPM Mathematics can test any syllabus topic.
