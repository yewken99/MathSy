# Formula Sheet Guide
**Curriculum:** SPM Mathematics  
**Purpose:** Quick reference guide for formulas commonly provided in the SPM Mathematics formula sheet.

## How to Use This Guide
This file is not meant to replace chapter notes. It is a quick reference to help students recognise:
- what each formula is used for,
- when to apply it,
- what each symbol means,
- common mistakes to avoid.

Use this guide when the question gives values and asks for calculation involving algebra, geometry, statistics, probability, transformation, matrices, or financial mathematics.

---

# 1. Numbers and Operations

## Laws of Indices

### Multiplication of Same Base
\[
a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}
\]

**Use when:** multiplying powers with the same base.

**Example:**
\[
x^2 \times x^5 = x^{2+5}=x^7
\]

### Division of Same Base
\[
a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}
\]

**Use when:** dividing powers with the same base.

**Example:**
\[
x^7 \div x^3 = x^{7-3}=x^4
\]

### Power of a Power
\[
(a^m)^n = a^{mn}
\]

**Use when:** a power is raised to another power.

**Example:**
\[
(x^2)^3=x^6
\]

### Fractional Index
\[
a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}
\]

**Use when:** changing between index form and surd/root form.

### Negative Index
\[
a^{-m} = \frac{1}{a^m}
\]

**Use when:** simplifying expressions with negative powers.

### Common Mistakes
- Do not add powers when the bases are different.
- Do not multiply the powers for \(a^m \times a^n\); add them.
- Negative index does not make the value negative. It means reciprocal.

---

## Simple Interest

\[
I = Prt
\]

where:
- \(I\) = interest,
- \(P\) = principal,
- \(r\) = annual interest rate in decimal,
- \(t\) = time in years.

**Use when:** the question says simple interest.

### Common Mistakes
- Convert percentage to decimal first.
  \[
  1.5\% = 0.015
  \]
- Time must be in years unless the question states otherwise.

---

## Maturity Value

\[
MV = P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}
\]

where:
- \(MV\) = maturity value,
- \(P\) = principal,
- \(r\) = annual interest rate in decimal,
- \(n\) = number of compounding periods per year,
- \(t\) = time in years.

**Use when:** compound interest is involved.

### Common Mistakes
- Use this for compound interest, not simple interest.
- If compounded monthly, \(n=12\).
- If compounded yearly, \(n=1\).

---

## Total Repayment

\[
A = P + Prt
\]

or

\[
A = P(1+rt)
\]

where:
- \(A\) = total repayment,
- \(P\) = loan amount or principal,
- \(r\) = interest rate,
- \(t\) = time.

**Use when:** calculating the total amount to be repaid under simple interest.

# 2. Relationship and Algebra

## Distance Between Two Points

\[
\text{Distance}=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}
\]

**Use when:** finding the distance between two coordinates.

### Common Mistakes
- Do not forget the square root.
- Be careful with negative coordinates.

---

## Midpoint

\[
(x,y)=\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)
\]

**Use when:** finding the middle point between two coordinates.

---

## Average Speed

\[
\text{Average speed}=\frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Total time}}
\]

**Use when:** the question asks for average speed for the whole journey.

### Common Mistakes
- Use total distance, not displacement.
- Include stopping time if it is part of the total time.
- Convert units if needed.

---

## Gradient Between Two Points

\[
m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}
\]

**Use when:** finding the gradient/slope of a straight line.

### Common Mistakes
- Use the same order for numerator and denominator.
- If \(x_2-x_1=0\), the line is vertical and the gradient is undefined.

---

## Gradient Using Intercepts

\[
m=-\frac{\text{y-intercept}}{\text{x-intercept}}
\]

**Use when:** a straight line is given by its x-intercept and y-intercept.

---

## Inverse of a \(2 \times 2\) Matrix

For

\[
A=
\begin{bmatrix}
a & b\\
c & d
\end{bmatrix}
\]

\[
A^{-1}=
\frac{1}{ad-bc}
\begin{bmatrix}
d & -b\\
-c & a
\end{bmatrix}
\]

where:

\[
ad-bc \ne 0
\]

**Use when:** finding the inverse matrix or solving simultaneous linear equations using matrices.

### Common Mistakes
- Swap \(a\) and \(d\).
- Change the signs of \(b\) and \(c\).
- Do not use the formula if \(ad-bc=0\).

# 3. Measurement and Geometry

## Pythagoras Theorem

\[
c^2=a^2+b^2
\]

where \(c\) is the hypotenuse.

**Use when:** finding a missing side in a right-angled triangle.

### Common Mistakes
- Only use this for right-angled triangles.
- The hypotenuse is always the longest side.

---

## Sum of Interior Angles of a Polygon

\[
\text{Sum of interior angles}=(n-2)\times180^\circ
\]

where \(n\) is the number of sides.

**Use when:** finding total interior angles of a polygon.

---

## Circle Circumference

\[
C=\pi d
\]

or

\[
C=2\pi r
\]

where:
- \(d\) = diameter,
- \(r\) = radius.

**Use when:** finding the distance around a circle.

---

## Area of Circle

\[
A=\pi r^2
\]

**Use when:** finding the area inside a circle.

### Common Mistakes
- Use radius, not diameter.
- If diameter is given, divide by 2 first.

---

## Arc Length

\[
\frac{\text{Arc length}}{2\pi r}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}
\]

or

\[
\text{Arc length}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times2\pi r
\]

**Use when:** finding the length of part of a circle.

---

## Area of Sector

\[
\frac{\text{Area of sector}}{\pi r^2}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}
\]

or

\[
\text{Area of sector}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\pi r^2
\]

**Use when:** finding the area of a sector.

---

## Area of Kite

\[
\text{Area of kite}=\frac{1}{2}\times\text{product of two diagonals}
\]

or

\[
A=\frac{1}{2}d_1d_2
\]

**Use when:** the diagonals of a kite are given.

---

## Area of Trapezium

\[
\text{Area of trapezium}
=
\frac{1}{2}\times\text{sum of two parallel sides}\times\text{height}
\]

or

\[
A=\frac{1}{2}(a+b)h
\]

**Use when:** finding the area of a trapezium with two parallel sides.

---

## Surface Area of Cylinder

\[
\text{Surface area of cylinder}=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh
\]

**Use when:** finding the total surface area of a closed cylinder.

### Common Mistakes
- \(2\pi r^2\) is for the two circular faces.
- \(2\pi rh\) is for the curved surface area.

---

## Surface Area of Cone

\[
\text{Surface area of cone}=\pi r^2+\pi rs
\]

where:
- \(r\) = radius,
- \(s\) = slant height.

**Use when:** finding total surface area of a cone.

### Common Mistakes
- Do not use vertical height for \(s\).
- \(s\) is the slant height.

---

## Surface Area of Sphere

\[
\text{Surface area of sphere}=4\pi r^2
\]

---

## Volume of Prism

\[
\text{Volume of prism}=\text{cross sectional area}\times\text{height}
\]

or

\[
V=Ah
\]

**Use when:** the solid has a uniform cross-section.

---

## Volume of Cylinder

\[
V=\pi r^2h
\]

---

## Volume of Cone

\[
V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2h
\]

---

## Volume of Sphere

\[
V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3
\]

---

## Volume of Pyramid

\[
V=\frac{1}{3}\times\text{base area}\times\text{height}
\]

---

# 4. Transformation

## Scale Factor

\[
k=\frac{PA'}{PA}
\]

where:
- \(P\) = centre of enlargement,
- \(A\) = object point,
- \(A'\) = image point.

**Use when:** describing or calculating enlargement.

### Common Mistakes
- Measure from the centre of enlargement.
- Image distance is divided by object distance.

---

## Area of Image Under Enlargement

\[
\text{Area of image}=k^2\times\text{Area of object}
\]

**Use when:** finding area after enlargement.

### Common Mistakes
- Length changes by \(k\).
- Area changes by \(k^2\).
- If \(k\) is negative, \(k^2\) is still positive.

---

# 5. Sets

## Number of Elements in Union

\[
n(A\cup B)=n(A)+n(B)-n(A\cap B)
\]

**Use when:** finding the total number of elements in \(A\) or \(B\).

### Common Mistakes
- Subtract the intersection because it is counted twice.

---

## Complement of a Set

\[
n(A')=n(\xi)-n(A)
\]

where:
- \(\xi\) = universal set,
- \(A'\) = complement of \(A\).

---

## De Morgan's Law

\[
n(A'\cap B')=n((A\cup B)')
\]

\[
n(A'\cup B')=n((A\cap B)')
\]

**Use when:** working with complements of combined sets.

---

# 6. Statistics and Probability

## Mean for Ungrouped Data

\[
\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x}{N}
\]

where:
- \(\bar{x}\) = mean,
- \(\sum x\) = sum of all data values,
- \(N\) = number of data values.

---

## Mean for Frequency Data

\[
\bar{x}=\frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}
\]

where:
- \(f\) = frequency,
- \(x\) = data value or midpoint.

### Common Mistakes
- For grouped data, \(x\) is usually the midpoint.
- Do not divide by number of classes; divide by \(\sum f\).

---

## Variance for Ungrouped Data

\[
\sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2}{N}
\]

or

\[
\sigma^2=\frac{\sum x^2}{N}-\bar{x}^2
\]

**Use when:** finding how spread out ungrouped data is.

---

## Variance for Frequency Data

\[
\sigma^2=\frac{\sum f(x-\bar{x})^2}{\sum f}
\]

or

\[
\sigma^2=\frac{\sum fx^2}{\sum f}-\bar{x}^2
\]

**Use when:** data values have frequencies or grouped data is given.

### Common Mistakes
- \(\sum fx^2\) means add \(f \times x^2\).
- It does not mean \((fx)^2\).

---

## Standard Deviation for Ungrouped Data

\[
\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x-\bar{x})^2}{N}}
\]

or

\[
\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum x^2}{N}-\bar{x}^2}
\]

---

## Standard Deviation for Frequency Data

\[
\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum f(x-\bar{x})^2}{\sum f}}
\]

or

\[
\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum fx^2}{\sum f}-\bar{x}^2}
\]

### Common Mistakes
- Standard deviation is the square root of variance.
- Variance has squared units, but standard deviation has the original unit.
- Smaller standard deviation means more consistent data.

---

## Probability of an Event

\[
P(A)=\frac{n(A)}{n(S)}
\]

where:
- \(n(A)\) = number of outcomes in event \(A\),
- \(n(S)\) = total number of outcomes in the sample space.

---

## Complement Probability

\[
P(A')=1-P(A)
\]

**Use when:** finding the probability that event \(A\) does not happen.

---

## Meaning of “And”

\[
P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A\cap B)
\]

**Use when:** both events happen.

---

## Probability of Independent Events

\[
P(A\cap B)=P(A)\times P(B)
\]

**Use when:** event \(A\) and event \(B\) are independent.

### Common Mistakes
- Only use this multiplication rule directly when events are independent.
- If events are dependent, the second probability changes.

---

## Meaning of “Or”

\[
P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A\cup B)
\]

**Use when:** at least one of the events happens.

---

## Addition Rule for Probability

\[
P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)
\]

**Use when:** finding the probability of \(A\) or \(B\), especially when the events overlap.

### Common Mistakes
- If events overlap, subtract \(P(A\cap B)\).
- If events are mutually exclusive, then \(P(A\cap B)=0\).

| Compound interest | \(MV=P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\) |
| Circle area | \(\pi r^2\) |

| Arc length | \(\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times2\pi r\) |

# 8. Common Exam Mistakes

## Units
Always check units before substituting into a formula.

Examples:
- km and m must not be mixed.
- minutes must be converted to hours if speed is in km h\(^{-1}\).
- percentage must be converted to decimal for interest.

## Radius and Diameter
If diameter is given:

\[
r=\frac{d}{2}
\]

Do not substitute diameter directly into formulas that use \(r\).

## Probability
- “And” usually means intersection.
- “Or” usually means union.
- For independent events, multiply.
- For overlapping events, subtract the intersection.

## Statistics
- For grouped data, use midpoint as \(x\).
- \(\sum fx^2\neq(\sum fx)^2\).
- Standard deviation is the square root of variance.

## Transformation
- Area changes by \(k^2\), not \(k\).
- Scale factor must be measured from the centre of enlargement.

## Matrices
- Inverse exists only if \(ad-bc\neq0\).
- \(A^{-1}\) does not mean \(\frac{1}{A}\).
