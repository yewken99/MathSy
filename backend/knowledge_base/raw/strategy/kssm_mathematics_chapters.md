# SPM Mathematics Chapter Map
**Subject / Subjek:** Mathematics / Matematik  
**Curriculum / Kurikulum:** KSSM  
**Purpose / Tujuan:** Help students identify Form 1 to Form 5 Mathematics chapters in English and Bahasa Melayu, and guide the chatbot when students ask about a form/chapter.

## How to Use This Guide
This guide is a chapter index, not a full set of notes. Use it when a student asks things like:
- Please explain Form 3 Chapter 2.
- Tolong terangkan Tingkatan 3 Bab 2.
- What chapter is Standard Form?
- Bentuk Piawai bab berapa?
- What should I revise before Form 5 Variation?

When a student asks for a form and chapter number, identify the bilingual chapter title first, then give a short overview and suggest related prerequisite chapters.

# 2. Student-Friendly Chapter Explanations / Penerangan Ringkas Bab

## Form 1 / Tingkatan 1

### Form 1 Chapter 1: Rational Numbers / Tingkatan 1 Bab 1: Nombor Nisbah
**English:** This chapter introduces positive and negative numbers, fractions, decimals and operations involving rational numbers.
**Bahasa Melayu:** Bab ini memperkenalkan nombor positif dan negatif, pecahan, perpuluhan dan operasi yang melibatkan nombor nisbah.
**Useful foundation for / Asas berguna untuk:** Form 3 Standard Form, financial mathematics, statistics

### Form 1 Chapter 2: Factors and Multiples / Tingkatan 1 Bab 2: Faktor dan Gandaan
**English:** This chapter covers factors, multiples, prime numbers, highest common factor and lowest common multiple.
**Bahasa Melayu:** Bab ini merangkumi faktor, gandaan, nombor perdana, faktor sepunya terbesar dan gandaan sepunya terkecil.

### Form 1 Chapter 3: Squares, Square Roots, Cubes and Cube Roots / Tingkatan 1 Bab 3: Kuasa Dua, Punca Kuasa Dua, Kuasa Tiga dan Punca Kuasa Tiga
**English:** This chapter introduces square numbers, square roots, cube numbers and cube roots.
**Bahasa Melayu:** Bab ini memperkenalkan nombor kuasa dua, punca kuasa dua, nombor kuasa tiga dan punca kuasa tiga.

### Form 1 Chapter 4: Ratios, Rates and Proportions / Tingkatan 1 Bab 4: Nisbah, Kadar dan Kadaran
**English:** This chapter teaches comparison between quantities using ratios, rates and proportions.
**Bahasa Melayu:** Bab ini mengajar perbandingan antara kuantiti menggunakan nisbah, kadar dan kadaran.

# 5. Important Chatbot Behaviour / Tingkah Laku Chatbot yang Dicadangkan

When a student asks for a form and chapter number:
1. Identify the chapter title in English and Bahasa Melayu.
2. Give a short overview of the chapter.
3. Mention key concepts.
4. Mention useful prerequisite chapters.
5. If full chapter notes are available, continue with the detailed explanation.
6. If full notes are not available, give a foundation-level explanation and ask which subtopic the student wants to learn.

Example:
> Form 3 Chapter 2 is Standard Form, or Bentuk Piawai. It is about writing very large and very small numbers using powers of 10. Before learning it, revise indices, especially powers of 10.

Contoh:
> Tingkatan 3 Bab 2 ialah Bentuk Piawai, atau Standard Form dalam Bahasa Inggeris. Bab ini berkaitan cara menulis nombor yang sangat besar dan sangat kecil menggunakan kuasa 10. Sebelum belajar bab ini, ulang kaji indeks, terutamanya kuasa 10.
